Overview

What RHEA Kernel is.

RHEA Kernel is an experimental AI-efficiency project focused on reducing computational, token, latency and energy cost in LLM-based workloads while preserving output quality.

The project remains under active private development and experimental validation. The public website and repository document selected outcomes and project records rather than the mechanism used to produce them.

Documentation principle. Public RHEA Kernel material is designed to establish what was measured, under what stated scope, and which private artifacts were frozen — not to provide enough information to reconstruct the system.

Validation model

How to read the public evidence.

RHEA Kernel publishes benchmark-specific outcomes. A result should always be interpreted together with its model, workload, validation size, quality conditions and measurement method.

  • Controlled baseline. Reported reductions are expressed relative to the comparison baseline used in the stated experiment.
  • Quality preservation. Public records report coverage, accuracy or output parity where those checks were part of the validation.
  • Cross-model replication. Repeating a validation pattern on additional model families supports reproducibility within those tested cases; it does not prove universal behavior.
  • Paired energy campaigns. Later energy experiments compare paired measurements under separately defined workload profiles rather than treating all profiles as one universal percentage.
  • Functional validation. Functional Freeze evidence refers to correctness and recorded behavior of the exact frozen runtime. It is distinct from an Energy Freeze.
No universal savings claim. Published percentages are specific to their stated experiments and must not be treated as guaranteed savings for every model, workload, company, dataset or hardware platform.
Terminology

Public validation glossary.

Baseline
The comparison condition used in a controlled experiment. Public reductions are reported relative to that stated baseline.
Coverage
Whether the benchmark's required cases were successfully handled under the recorded validation criteria.
Accuracy
Whether outputs met the benchmark's correctness requirement under the recorded validation procedure.
Output parity
A recorded check that the compared outputs satisfy the parity requirement defined for that validation campaign.
Wall time
End-to-end elapsed workload time measured for the stated benchmark run.
System-wide energy estimate
An energy estimate derived from sampled macOS system-wide telemetry in the published experiments. It is not a calibrated external power-meter measurement and is not process-exclusive.
Functional Freeze
A formal decision that the exact recorded runtime has met the stated functional validation gate. It does not automatically transfer earlier energy results.
Energy Freeze
A separate energy-validation status. Functional Freeze and Energy Freeze are intentionally treated as distinct claims.
Frozen artifact
A retained private package whose exact contents are fixed and can later be checked against its recorded cryptographic fingerprint.
SHA-256 fingerprint
A cryptographic digest used as an integrity reference. Publishing a hash does not disclose the underlying private artifact.

Artifact integrity

Why fingerprints are published.

Selected private RHEA Kernel artifacts are retained outside the public repository. Their SHA-256 fingerprints can be published without exposing those artifacts.

SHA-256Cryptographic fingerprint format used in the public integrity records.
PrivateThe underlying frozen source packages and evidence bundles are not published by those fingerprint records.
VerifiableA retained copy can later be hashed and compared with the public fingerprint to check exact identity.
Important limitation. A published artifact fingerprint is an integrity record only. The public repository explicitly states that it is not, by itself, a patent filing, statutory copyright registration, qualified electronic timestamp or legal opinion regarding priority or ownership.
Disclosure boundary

What this documentation does not disclose.

RHEA Kernel is proprietary. Technical implementation and unpublished supporting material remain private, and publication of the public repository does not grant a license to unpublished RHEA Kernel materials.

  • No source release. RHEA Kernel is not released as open-source software.
  • No reconstruction guide. Public documentation is not intended to provide information sufficient to reconstruct the system.
  • No universal performance guarantee. Experimental figures remain tied to the stated validation context.
  • No implied license. Publication of selected records does not grant rights to unpublished proprietary material.
