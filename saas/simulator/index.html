RHEA Savings Simulator.

Estimate how much RHEA could save on your LLM workload using measured operating data from your own system — not a historical RHEA benchmark percentage.

How this estimate works. The calculator measures the cost of work in your own LLM workload that you identify as repeated, equivalent or otherwise reusable without a fresh full-model computation. It then deducts attributable RHEA operating costs and the applicable performance fee. Actual commercial Verified Net Savings are established only after RHEA validates eligible work and the agreed quality gate is maintained.
