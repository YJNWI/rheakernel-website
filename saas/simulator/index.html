RHEA Savings Simulator.

Estimate how much RHEA could save on your LLM workload using your own measured usage and cost data. The calculator does not use RHEA's historical benchmark reduction percentages.

What the calculator measures. You identify work in your existing LLM workload that is genuinely repeated or equivalent and therefore potentially reusable without a fresh full-model computation. The simulator prices that measured subset, deducts attributable RHEA operating cost, applies the current performance-fee terms, and projects the result over the time period you choose. Actual commercial savings remain subject to deployment-time verification and the agreed quality gate.

Method, assumptions & month-by-month table

This calculator uses customer-provided measured workload data. It does not use historical RHEA benchmark percentages. Simple mode assumes privately reusable and Shared-eligible requests have the same average cost and token volume as the overall workload. Advanced mode prices each subset from its own measured token profile. Private plan savings come only from the customer's own repeated/equivalent work. Shared + Private can add a Shared Network component only from a privacy-safe monthly aggregate snapshot; no company-level values are exposed to this page.

The estimate deliberately excludes additional savings that cannot be derived from the supplied data. Actual RHEA deployment may classify fewer requests as eligible, and commercial savings require equivalent workload and the agreed quality gate. The simulator is not a guarantee or binding quote.
