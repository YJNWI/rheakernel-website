Commercial model

RHEA earns when the customer saves.

Under the standard commercial model, RHEA receives 25% of positive Verified Net Savings generated by RHEA. The customer retains the remaining 75%. Billing is monthly and in arrears, after the measurement period has been completed and the applicable savings have been verified.

How savings are verified

Equivalent workload. Agreed quality. Reviewable economics.

RHEA does not define savings by simply comparing two monthly bills. Savings are measured on an agreed equivalent workload, under an agreed quality gate, using a like-for-like baseline and RHEA-enabled comparison.

01 · Baseline

Baseline Equivalent Cost

The cost of executing the agreed workload under the baseline condition, with the agreed workload scope, measurement period and required quality level.

02 · Comparability

Equivalent workload

Baseline and RHEA-enabled measurements must represent comparable work. Changes in workload volume, scope or required quality must not be misattributed to RHEA.

03 · Quality

Quality gate

Cost reduction qualifies as commercial savings only when the agreed quality requirement is maintained. Lower cost achieved by degrading required quality does not qualify.

04 · Before overhead

Gross savings

Baseline Equivalent Cost minus the corresponding RHEA-enabled workload cost for the agreed equivalent workload.

05 · Attributable cost

Incremental RHEA costs

Agreed incremental costs attributable to operating RHEA for the measured workload are deducted before the commercial saving is determined.

06 · Commercial basis

Verified Net Savings

The positive economic saving remaining after the RHEA-enabled workload cost and attributable incremental RHEA costs are deducted from the equivalent baseline.

Verified Net Savings = Baseline Equivalent Cost − RHEA-enabled workload cost − incremental costs attributable to running RHEA RHEA Performance Fee = max(0, Verified Net Savings) × contractual performance-fee rate
Quality is part of the calculation. If the agreed quality gate is not satisfied, the corresponding cost reduction does not qualify as Verified Net Savings.
Reviewable measurement. The launch service is intended to provide customer-facing measurement records that can be reviewed and exported for reconciliation or audit without exposing RHEA Kernel's proprietary implementation. FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers about pricing, savings verification, billing, quality requirements and data boundaries for RHEA Kernel as a Service.

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. RHEA Kernel as a Service has no upfront payment, setup fee or access fee. The customer receives the service first, and RHEA's performance fee is calculated only after Verified Net Savings have been generated and verified for the measurement period.

How much does RHEA charge?

Under the standard commercial model, RHEA receives 25% of positive Verified Net Savings. The customer retains the remaining 75%. If Verified Net Savings are €0 or negative, RHEA's performance fee for that period is €0.

When am I billed?

Billing is monthly and in arrears. The measurement period is completed first, the applicable Verified Net Savings are determined, and the corresponding RHEA performance fee is then calculated.

What are Verified Net Savings?

Verified Net Savings are the positive economic saving attributable to RHEA for an agreed equivalent workload after accounting for the RHEA-enabled workload cost and agreed incremental costs attributable to operating RHEA.

Can you simply compare my AI bill before and after RHEA?

No. A lower monthly bill by itself does not establish RHEA savings. The comparison must use an agreed equivalent workload so that changes in workload volume, scope, required quality or other material conditions are not incorrectly attributed to RHEA.

What is the quality gate?

The quality gate is the agreed minimum output-quality requirement for the measured workload. A cost reduction qualifies as Verified Net Savings only when that requirement is maintained. A cheaper result achieved by degrading the agreed required quality is not treated as verified savings.

Do the benchmark results on rheakernel.com predict what my company will save?

No. Results published on the RHEA Kernel Results page are historical measurements from specific controlled experiments under their stated conditions. A customer's commercial savings are measured separately using that customer's own agreed workload, baseline, quality gate and attributable costs.

What are the Founding Customer terms?

The first global customer receives a 20% performance-fee rate for the first 12 months. Founding customers #2 and #3 receive a 22.5% rate for the first 12 months. After that period, the standard 25% rate applies. These are formal Founding Customer terms, not discretionary discounts.

Do Private and Shared Network + Private have different entry prices?

No. Neither service mode has an upfront access fee. They differ by knowledge scope and functionality, not by an initial entry charge.

Does Shared Network + Private expose my private company data to other customers?

No direct tenant-to-tenant access is intended. Private company material remains within its authorized private scope. Any shared knowledge layer is intended to use only material permitted for contribution and reuse under the applicable controls.

Can the savings calculation be reviewed or audited?

The commercial model is designed around reviewable measurement. RHEA intends to provide customer-facing records sufficient to reconcile the agreed baseline, measured workload costs, attributable incremental costs, quality-gate result, Verified Net Savings and resulting performance fee. Exact audit and export procedures will be defined in the commercial launch terms.

Does RHEA disclose how its internal technology produces the savings?

No. The commercial measurement methodology explains how outcomes and savings are evaluated. It does not disclose RHEA Kernel's proprietary internal algorithms, routing logic, thresholds, data structures or other reconstruction-enabling implementation details.
