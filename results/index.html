Interpretation

What the public evidence supports.

Within the stated experiments, RHEA Kernel has demonstrated reductions in complete model invocations, token use, elapsed workload time and measured system-wide energy while preserving the required output quality.

Scope matters.

The project does not claim that the same percentages apply to every AI system, model, workload, company or hardware platform. Results on this page are tied to the specific experiments and measurement procedures described in the public record.
